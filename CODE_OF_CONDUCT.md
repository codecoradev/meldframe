# Code of Conduct

Meldframe is worked on by a small team and by AI agents. We want it to stay a place where good work is easy to do.

## The rules, briefly

- **Be respectful.** Disagree with the idea, not the person. Rudeness, condescension and personal attacks are not acceptable.
- **Assume good faith.** Most miscommunication is not malice. Ask before you escalate.
- **Stay on topic.** Issues and pull requests are about Meldframe. Review the change, not the author.
- **No harassment.** Insults, slurs, sustained disruption, sexualised comments, doxxing and threats are not tolerated, against anyone, anywhere inside the project.
- **Keep private things private.** Do not share credentials, customer data or non-public plans outside the project.
- **Agents follow the same rules.** Text an agent writes in an issue, a review or a pull request is held to the same standard as text a person writes, and the person who runs the agent is responsible for it.

This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, commits and every other space the project uses.

## Enforcement

Report a violation, or something you experienced, to the repository owner, privately and with links and context. The owner may, at their discretion: ask for a change, remove a comment, limit participation, or remove someone's access. Reports are handled in confidence, and retaliation against someone who reports in good faith is itself a violation.
